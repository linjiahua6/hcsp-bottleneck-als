Place your own figures here, then compile main.tex with XeLaTeX.

Default filenames and maximum fitted sizes:
  motivation.pdf    297 x 146 mm
  method.pdf        522 x 258 mm
  results.pdf       579 x 173 mm
  comparison.pdf    529 x 118 mm
  qualitative.pdf   229 x 150 mm
  qr-code.pdf       123 x 123 mm

You can also use PNG or JPG: update the corresponding filename in main.tex.
PDF is recommended for plots, diagrams and QR codes to preserve vector detail.
Images keep their aspect ratio and are never stretched or cropped.
The template does not invent experimental data or generate a real QR code.
